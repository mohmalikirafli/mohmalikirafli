## About Me

I am a **Master of Public Health student** specializing in **Biostatistics and Health Data Science**. My work sits at the intersection of public health, quantitative research, and reproducible analytics.

I use statistical reasoning and data science to transform complex health data into evidence that can support research, policy, and decision-making. My interests include epidemiology, statistical modeling, systematic reviews and meta-analysis, machine learning, and data visualization.

- 🔬 Focus: **Biostatistics, Epidemiology, and Health Data Science**
- 📊 Work: **Data analysis, statistical modeling, and evidence synthesis**
- 🧠 Current interests: **Advanced analytics, machine learning, and reproducible research**
- 🤝 Open to: **Research collaborations and data-focused opportunities**
